      Frequently asked questions

      How fast do you reply to messages?

      Most queries are answered within 2–4 hours during business hours, and within 24 hours on weekends and holidays.

      Can I get support for an existing booking?

      Yes — mention your booking ID in the message form or call our support line for faster resolution.

      Do you offer refunds if my trip is cancelled?

      Absolutely. Our cancel-and-refund policy is outlined at checkout, and most refunds are processed within 5–7 working days.

      Is phone support available on weekends?

      Yes, our live support team is available every day from 8 AM to 11 PM, including weekends and public holidays.
